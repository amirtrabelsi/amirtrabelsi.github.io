Make robots more efficient in challenging environments

I am a Research Engineer in Medical Robotics specializing in the development of advanced multi-robot platforms for minimally invasive surgery. I hold a PhD focused on the optimal design and kinematic redundancy management of surgical robotic systems. My expertise bridges the gap between complex hardware-software integration and real-time control algorithms, specializing in ROS2, digital twins, and collaborative robotics to enhance surgical precision in challenging environments.

Featured Projects

Implementation of real-time control algorithms for macro-mini robot.

ROS2 EtherCAT C++

Design and development of a robotic assistant for percutaneous interventions, including real-time control algorithms for macro-mini systems at LS2N.

Real-time collision avoidance based on kinematic redundancy management

ROS Optimization Redundancy management

Development of a real-time obstacles avoidance algorithm using kinematic redundancy management for serial robots.

Design and integration of a tool holder system for surgical applications

SolidWorks micro-ROS Medical tools

Designed and prototyped a specialized robotic end-effector for laparoscopic surgery, incorporating a modular tool-changing interface. Integrated motion control to manage instrument action and self-rotation, optimizing the robot’s dexterity and orientation within constrained surgical workspaces.

Generating Robot Capability Maps

Malab ROS Optimization

Development of an algorithm for generating capability maps for serial robots to represent the performance of a robot's workspace and encode the accessibility and dexterity of the end effector. The algorithm was validated on a Franka Emika robot and applied to optimize the placement of a surgical camera holder in a multi-robot surgical platform.

Franka Emika Simulator (CSP)

MATLAB Simulink Robot Dynamics

Developed a high-fidelity simulation environment using Simscape to model the kinematics and dynamics of the Franka Emika robot. Validated the platform for medical human-robot collaboration (HRC) by analyzing joint torques and friction models to ensure safety and precision in shared workspaces.

Design of an actuator for an articulated arm

SolidWorks Harmonic Drive Power Train

Developed a fully integrated actuator solution for robotic arm joints, successfully combining harmonic reduction gearing, brushless motor technology, and precision position sensing into a compact mechanical design.
